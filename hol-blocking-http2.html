🎯 HOL Blocking Demo - HTTP/2

HTTP/2 Multiplexing (No HOL Blocking)

GET /api/slow

This demo demonstrates HTTP/2 multiplexing, which eliminates Head-of-Line (HOL) blocking. With HTTP/2, multiple requests can be sent simultaneously over a single connection without queuing, unlike HTTP/1.1's 6-connection limit.

💡 HTTP/2 Advantage: All 30 requests can be sent concurrently over one connection using multiplexing. You'll notice they all complete in roughly the same time, without the batching effect seen in HTTP/1.1.
